May allah save me. 让安拉救救我吧。
May Allah grant me salvation. 愿安拉赐我救赎。

When one is in the throes of suffering, it's only natural that thoughts turn to religion.
当人在痛苦的时候，想到宗教方面的东西是很自然的事情。

Actually, my view is that when a person cannot find meaning in their life, religion may well serve as a source of solace—a way to give the spirit something to hold onto.
