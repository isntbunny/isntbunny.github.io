## 虚拟语气用于非真实条件句（主观意愿）
从句中提出一种与客观现实不相符或根本不可能存在的条件，主句会产生一种不可能获得的结果。
### 与现在事实相反
谓语格式：

条件句：过去式

主句：would do

- ~~If I see the rabbit now, i will bite him.~~ If I saw the rabbit now, i would bite him.
- If i were you, i would give this video a thumbs-up.
- If i got married in the UK, i would expect a present, but if I got married in Italy, I would have to give a present.

在考试中，阅读中，只是看见句中有过去式，咱们还不能仅仅根据这一信息就直接推断他是过去发生的事情

### 与将来事实相反（有的语法体系会将其说成条件语气：也就是可能性很小）

主句：would do

条件句：

1. 不太可能，但理论上不是完全没戏：（与“现在”形式差不多）
- If I **had** a million dollars next year, I would travel. 
- If everyone **decided** to be clone, there would be a population explosion.
  - 这句话为什么是与将来事实相反？因为decide这个动作，在逻辑上永远指向“将来”。
  - 类似词语还有：plan intend mean（意欲）, wish hope expect, suggest propose recommend, promise guarantee swear, arrange shedule appoint.
- 其实【现在】例句中的第三个也可以理解为将来。
1. 幻想时间。绝对不可能，天方夜谭：
- If I <u>were to</u> **have** a billion dollars next year, I would travel to mars.
- If things <u>were to</u> **be** done twice, all would be wise.
1. 如果真发生了会很出乎意料 - should+动原（这里的should是shall的过去式）：
- If it should rain tomorrow, we wold cancel the picnic.
- if i see the rabbit ~~now~~ tomorrow, i ~~will~~ would bite him.

### 与过去事实相反

如果我说 if i saw the rabbit a few days ago...
岂不是和1的虚拟语气形式一样了吗？
所以要用这种形式
条件句：had done
主句：would have done
- ~~if i saw the rabbit a few days ago, i would bite him.~~
- if i **had seen** the rabbit a few days ago, i **would have bitten** him.

## 与条件状语从句的区别

### 条件状语从句（真实条件句）
***主将从现***：如果条件满足，将来很可能发生
If I study hard, I will get good grades.
If you don't hurry, you will miss the bus.

### 虚拟语气（非真实条件句）
***时态倒退***：表示假设、幻想或不可能。

## 虚拟语气用于：愿望/请求/建议/命令
### wish 
wish是希望，hope也是希望
但wish可能性小，hope比较中性

所以，wish
对现在的愿望： wish did
- i wish that i were a rabbit
- if i were a rabbit, i would eat a carrot
- i wish you were here
对过去的愿望：wish had/would have done
- i wish i had eaten the carrot
对将来的愿望：wish would/could/might/should do
- i wish i could eat the carrort

### would rather
i would rather thar you didnt eat the carrot.
i would rather that you hadnt eaten the carrot.

### demand, suggest, insist, order + should do
- i demand you ~~should~~(省略) give me a carrot.
- i demanded you ~~should~~ give me a carrot.

- i suggest you should eat a carrot.
- it is suggested that you should eat a carrot.
- the suggestion is that you should eat a carrot.
- it is time that you ate a carrot.
