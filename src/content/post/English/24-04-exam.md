## 一、动词短语与固定搭配
- `in the name of sb.`
  以xx的名义
  *Don't make unreasonable demands in the name of friendship.*
- `watch out` – 当心
  - *Watch out for the slippery floor.*
  - *Watch out for anyone who asks for your password in the name of customer service.*
- `watch over` – 监视，照管
  - *Can you watch over my baby while I go to the store?*
  - *I'll stay here and watch over things while you're away.*
- `keep...in mind` – 记住
- `pull over` – 靠边停车
- `pull up` – 停车；拔起
- `roll down` – 摇下（车窗）
- `blow out` – 吹灭；爆胎
- `speed up how long it takes to do something` – 加快做某事所需的时间
