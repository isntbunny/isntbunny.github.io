【语法笔记】英语兔 - 动词 - 虚拟语气

Published 2026-08-30
Reading 3 min read
Words 552字
  • 动词时态
  • 动词语气
    • 陈述语气 (indicative mood)
      • The rabbit ate the carrot
      • Did the rabbit eat the carrot?
      • Was the rabbit hungry
    • 祈使语气 (imperative mood) ：用第二人称，用一般现在时态，去掉你，并保证动词原形
      • Rabbit, you eat the carrot.
      • You are quiet! -> Be quiet!
    • 虚拟语气 (subjunctive mood)
  • 非谓语动词

虚拟语气描述与事实相悖的假设（主观意愿）

  1. 与现在事实相反 格式： 条件句：过去式 主句：would+动词原形

  • If I see the rabbit now, i will bite him. If I saw the rabbit now, i would bite him.

  • If i were you, i would give this video a thumbs-up.

在考试中，阅读中，只是看见句中有过去式，咱们还不能仅仅根据这一信息就直接推断他是过去发生的事情

  1. 与将来事实相反（有的语法体系会将其说成条件语气：也就是可能性很小） 那为什么我们有的时候还能看见if sb DOES sth呢？ 条件句：should+动原（这里的should是shall的过去式） 主句：would+动原
  • if i see the rabbit now tomorrow, i will would bite him.
  1. 与过去事实相反 ——如果我在后悔过去没做某件事呢？ 如果我说 if i saw the rabbit a few days ago… 岂不是和1的虚拟语气形式一样了吗？ 所以要用这种形式 条件句：had done 主句：would have done
  • if i saw the rabbit a few days ago, i would bite him.
  • if i had seen the rabbit a few days ago, i would have bitten him.

虚拟语气用于：愿望/请求/建议/命令

wish

wish是希望，hope也是希望 但wish可能性小，hope比较中性

所以，wish 对现在的愿望： wish did

  • i wish that i were a rabbit
  • if i were a rabbit, i would eat a carrot
  • i wish you were here 对过去的愿望：wish had/would have done
  • i wish i had eaten the carrot 对将来的愿望：wish would/could/might/should do
  • i wish i could eat the carrort

would rather

i would rather thar you didnt eat the carrot. i would rather that you hadnt eaten the carrot.

demand, suggest, insist, order + should do

  • i demand you should(省略) give me a carrot.

  • i demanded you should give me a carrot.

  • i suggest you should eat a carrot.

  • it is suggested that you should eat a carrot.

  • the suggestion is that you should eat a carrot.

  • it is time that you ate a carrot.
